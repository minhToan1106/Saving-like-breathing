Beat Yourself — Product Requirements

Bet on your own spending each morning, settle up each night, and beat yesterday's you. Hand-drawn, gothic, sepia.

Canonical document: devpost/prd.md. This page is a companion for reviewing it together.

What the scope said → what we know now

Morning

"pick a bet type, tap an amount"
One bet: "spend no more than", with buttons $2 · $3 · $5 · $10 · Custom.

Verdict

"compare night with morning balance"
Actual spent = Morning + Income today − Night, so getting paid can't hide overspending.

Losing

"tomorrow's stakes get harder"
A flat $0.50 penalty on tomorrow's limit, announced with a morning notice. A missed check-in also counts as a loss.

New in this build

A Diary (spending + income), a Streaks grid, a monthly goal, a 30-day grand achievement, and a day/night toggle.

The core journey

Dashed = optional or demo-only. Losing also carries tomorrow's penalty back to step 3.

Explore each screen

Home: morning bet

Top half: "Do you think you can beat yourself?" drops in. Bottom half: "Today I'll spend no more than…" $2 · $3 · $5 · $10 · Custom, then the morning balance. The monthly goal sits below.

States

First use: streak 0 and the hook. Day after a loss: a penalty notice appears before the bet. Invalid or empty input: a friendly message, and the bet isn't confirmed.

How we'll know it works
  • All five buttons visible, and the tapped one is marked
  • Custom lets you type an amount
  • After a loss: notice shown, limit = tapped − $0.50
  • Can't confirm without an amount and a valid balance

Home: night check-in and verdict

Unlocks at 7 PM, or right away with Skip to tonight. Enter the night balance and get the verdict.

States

Win: streak +1, achievement pop-up, unspent amount added to monthly savings. Lose: streak → 0, $0.50 penalty tomorrow. No check-in by midnight → loss.

How we'll know it works
  • $50 morning, +$100 income, $130 night, $3 limit → Lose
  • $50 morning, no income, $48 night, $3 limit → Win
  • Verdict appears right after entering the night balance

Diary

Top: this month's balance. Bottom: add entries as − spending or + income, and see the list. Today's income feeds the verdict. Spending entries are a record only.

States

Empty: a short prompt to add a first entry. Invalid amount: a friendly message.

How we'll know it works
  • $4 spending → balance drops by exactly $4
  • $100 income → balance rises $100 and counts in tonight's income
  • New entries appear in the list

Streaks

Activity grid: green winred lossgray no data, plus the current streak.

How we'll know it works
  • Today's square turns green or red after the verdict
  • Streak number matches Home
  • "Skip to 30 days of success" → 30 green squares + grand achievement, and monthly savings filled (monthly achievement if the goal is reached)

Everywhere

Left sidebar (Home · Diary · Streaks) and the top-right day/night toggle. Everything is remembered on this device after closing and reopening. One user, no login.

  • Toggle switches the whole look immediately
  • Reload keeps streak, history, diary, goal, and theme

Try the verdict

Check the formula with your own numbers: Actual spent = Morning + Income today − Night.

Building now vs. later

Building now

  • Sidebar + day/night toggle
  • Morning bet, monthly goal
  • Night check-in, verdict formula, $0.50 penalty
  • Streak, achievements (win · 30-day · monthly)
  • Diary (spending + income)
  • Streaks grid
  • Two demo skip buttons

Later

  • Login: needs accounts and a server
  • Font settings: cut to make room for the diary
  • Many devices: needs online storage + login
  • Real night notifications
  • Leaderboards, friends, pushups penalty, more achievements

Never: charging real money, gambling framing, bank connections, copied game assets.

Look and feel

Hand-drawn gothic, inspired by the feel of Don't Starve. Sepia and dark tones in several layers, paper texture, sketchy borders. It should look like real hand drawing, not an AI template. No copied game assets. Say "stakes," never "gambling."
